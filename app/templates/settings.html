Storage Tips

Freeing Outlook Storage

  1. Back up first: Go to the Dashboard and click "Backup Now"
  2. Empty Deleted Items: In Outlook, right-click "Deleted Items" → Empty Folder
  3. Clear Sent Items: Old sent emails often consume a lot of space
  4. Junk Email: Empty your Junk Email folder
  5. Large attachments: Sort by size and delete emails with large attachments (they are backed up here!)
